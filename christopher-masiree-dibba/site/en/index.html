Christopher Masiree Dibba is a businessman and investor, and the founder and chief executive of several companies. Ambitious and disciplined, he is driven by a desire to build, to invest and to create lasting opportunities.

He comes from the Dibbamasiree family, a respected family with royal roots: the grandson of a king, he is the heir to a lineage shaped by tradition and history. Of Senegalese and Gambian descent through his parents, he was born and raised in the United States, in Edina, Minnesota, where he began school at Concord Elementary School.

He later moved to Africa, where he spent several formative years of his youth. That time allowed him to reconnect deeply with his roots, to absorb African cultural traditions and values, and to deepen his faith.

Back in the United States, he continued his education at Edina Senior High School and then at the University of Minnesota, where he studied Computer Science and Business Management.

This journey between African and American cultures has shaped his character, his discipline and his outlook on the world. Drawing on this family, cultural and academic heritage, he has built an approach to life grounded in ambition, knowledge and the will to build a lasting future.

Vision and legacy

Building a lasting legacy

Christopher Masiree Dibba aims to see his companies keep expanding internationally, to form new strategic partnerships and to create more jobs.

Beyond financial success, he wants to build a united family, take care of those close to him and contribute positively to society. For him, true success is also measured by the values one embodies, the people one helps and the impact one leaves behind.

I want to be remembered not only for what I have built, but above all for the man I have been, the actions I have taken and the positive impact I have left on the lives of others.

Christopher Masiree Dibba
